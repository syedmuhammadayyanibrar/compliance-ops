# Customer Support AI System (CS-AI-Core)

## Overview
CS-AI-Core is an enterprise customer assistance generative AI system developed by Nexus Technologies. The system leverages fine-tuned Large Language Models to assist customer support representatives with ticket classification, automated draft response generation, and sentiment analysis across retail channels.

## System Characteristics
- **System ID**: `customer-support-ai`
- **System Type**: `generative_ai`
- **Application Domain**: Enterprise B2B Customer Support & Ticketing Automation
- **Risk Classification**: High Risk (per EU AI Act Article 6 Annex III - critical business decision support & automated customer communication)
- **Deployment Status**: Pre-deployment assessment stage
- **Model Architecture**: Multi-stage Transformer pipeline with Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) over corporate knowledge bases
