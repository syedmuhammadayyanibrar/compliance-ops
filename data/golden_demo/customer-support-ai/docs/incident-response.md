# Serious Incident Notification & Response Procedure

## Framework Reference
EU AI Act Article 73 - Reporting of Serious Incidents

## Incident Classification
Incidents involving the CS-AI-Core are categorized into three severity levels:
- **Severity 1 (Critical)**: Unauthorized disclosure of sensitive customer data or system advice causing substantial financial loss.
- **Severity 2 (High)**: Systemic outage affecting over 20% of customer support workflows.
- **Severity 3 (Moderate)**: Isolated model misclassifications without regulatory or customer impact.

## Reporting Protocol
In accordance with EU AI Act Article 73, any serious incident must be immediately investigated by the AI Safety Officer and reported to the relevant market surveillance authority within 15 days of becoming aware of the incident.

## Incident Log
All incident records, forensic traces, and remediation actions are persisted in the central compliance audit archive for a minimum retention duration of 10 years.
