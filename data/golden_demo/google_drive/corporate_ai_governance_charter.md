## 1. Governance Principles
Nexus Technologies enforces responsible AI development across all production automated decision systems. In alignment with the EU AI Act (Regulation EU 2024/1689), high-risk AI deployments must adhere to:
1. Human Agency and Oversight (Article 14)
2. Technical Robustness and Safety (Article 15)
3. Privacy and Data Governance (Article 10)
4. Transparency (Article 13)
5. Diversity, Non-discrimination and Fairness (Article 10)

## 2. Mandatory Approval for External Ticketing
Any compliance gap remediation affecting customer-facing workflows requires explicit review and approval by the Designated AI Compliance Lead before creation of operational engineering tickets.
