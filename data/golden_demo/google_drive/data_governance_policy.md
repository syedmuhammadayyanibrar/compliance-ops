## Training & Validation Data Governance
1. **Sanitization**: Training corpora must be scrubbed of Direct Identifiers prior to ingestion.
2. **Bias Monitoring**: Dataset distributions across geographic customer demographics must be evaluated for statistical parity.
3. **Data Provenance**: Training datasets must maintain verifiable cryptographic provenance manifests detailing collection dates and source authorization.
